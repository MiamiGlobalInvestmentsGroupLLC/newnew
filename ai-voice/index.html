How this assistant works

Text source guidance is available immediately. Built-in neural voice samples play without an AI model download. Optional custom neural speech and speech recognition run in your browser after a model download. Optional conversational AI adds a local language model. Performance depends on your device; text remains available if local AI cannot run.

Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face. Your questions, microphone recordings and generated speech are processed on your device; this assistant does not upload or retain customer audio. Downloads may be cached by your browser. Conversation history stays in this tab until cleared. Contact forms have their own privacy terms.

AI responses may need confirmation. Prices, staffing, security requirements and launch dates should be agreed directly with MGI.
