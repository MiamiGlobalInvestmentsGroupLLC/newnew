The problem we are designing around

BUSINESS OPERATIONS ARE STILL TOO FRAGMENTED.

Growing companies often run customer communications, sales follow-up, scheduling, task handoffs, QA and reporting across separate tools, spreadsheets and manual routines. MGI is designing a product layer intended to make those workflows more connected, measurable and automatable.

01 / ENGAGE

AI-assisted customer engagement

Planned support for conversation assistance, appointment flows, follow-up and guided responses inside approved business rules.

02 / ORCHESTRATE

Workflow automation

Planned routing for work, tasks, handoffs, reminders and repetitive operating steps.

03 / OPERATE

Human + AI execution

A design direction that keeps people in the loop with approvals, escalation paths and operational ownership.

04 / MEASURE

Operational analytics

A unified view for activity, QA signals, workflow status and management reporting.

05 / CONNECT

API-first integrations

Designed around connection to CRM, helpdesk, communications and other systems instead of creating another isolated tool.

06 / SCALE

Cloud-native architecture

A modular, event-driven direction intended to support rapid iteration and future scale.

Cloud architecture evaluation

DESIGNED FOR SERVERLESS, EVENT-DRIVEN SCALE.

MGI is evaluating AWS as a potential core cloud environment for a future MVP and production architecture. The objective is a modular stack that can support rapid product iteration and scale if the product advances beyond design.

Potential AWS architecture

Generative AIAmazon Bedrock for model access and AI-enabled workflow capabilities.
Application APIsAWS Lambda + Amazon API Gateway for serverless business logic and integrations.
IdentityAmazon Cognito for user identity, authentication and role-aware access patterns.
Application dataAmazon DynamoDB and/or Amazon Aurora depending on validated data-access patterns.
Object storageAmazon S3 for files, exports and application objects where appropriate.
Events & visibilityAmazon EventBridge and CloudWatch for event-driven coordination, logs, metrics and operational visibility.

Architecture principles

API-first. Capabilities should connect to existing client systems.

Least privilege. Access should be scoped by role and workflow need.

Observable. Important automation and application events should be measurable and traceable.

Modular. Product capabilities should be independently evolvable as the concept is validated.

Current status: this is a pre-MVP product direction, not a publicly launched SaaS service. The AWS services above describe architecture under evaluation and are not a claim that these services are already deployed in production. Final service selection, product scope and launch timing remain subject to engineering validation and business decisions.
