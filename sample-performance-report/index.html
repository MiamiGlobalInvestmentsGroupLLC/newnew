Example weekly operations review

Coverage: Planned versus actual shifts, attendance exceptions and backup arrangements.

Production: Calls, conversations, tickets, tasks completed or appointments booked, depending on service.

Quality: Sample size, agreed QA checklist, coaching notes and repeat issues.

Business outcomes: Client-approved KPIs such as qualified meetings, resolution rates or follow-up accuracy; no guaranteed outcomes.

Escalations: Open issues, owners, deadlines and resolution status.

Next-week action plan: Training priorities, workflow changes and decisions required from the client.

What a real report includes

Reporting frequency, data sources, definitions, targets, system permissions and review ownership are established in the service agreement. Client-specific dashboards are shared through approved channels rather than publicly.
