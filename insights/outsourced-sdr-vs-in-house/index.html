Start with the sales motion, not the headcount

Both models can work. An internal SDR team gives you direct control over hiring, coaching and sales culture. Outsourced SDR capacity may reduce internal recruitment and management work, but only when responsibilities, tools and expectations are defined. Neither model guarantees qualified pipeline.

What to compare before buying

1. Total cost and contract terms

Compare salary and benefits, recruiting, onboarding, management time, tools and data costs against any outsourced monthly fees, setup charges, minimum commitments and exit terms. Ask whether you are buying a dedicated person, shared capacity or an outcome-based service; those models are not equivalent.

2. Qualification and meeting acceptance

Write down your ideal customer profile, exclusions, decision-maker criteria and what counts as an accepted meeting. Track meetings held and accepted by the sales team rather than counting calendar bookings alone.

3. Data and CRM ownership

Agree who provides target accounts, prospect data, approved messaging, the dialer and CRM access. Specify how dispositions, consent records, handoffs and reporting will be handled. Confirm applicable outreach and privacy requirements before launching.

4. Coaching and oversight

Clarify who reviews call quality, resolves objections, approves script changes and monitors escalation issues. Dedicated outsourced execution still needs client input on positioning and sales strategy.

5. Pilot and scale criteria

Use a scoped pilot to test process fit, data quality, reporting and handoffs. Review leading indicators and qualitative feedback before adding capacity.

A practical SDR outsourcing checklist

  • Document the ideal customer profile and exclusions
  • Agree what qualifies as a sales-ready meeting
  • Identify owners of data, tools, scripts and compliance
  • Define reporting cadence and escalation rules
  • Review held meetings and sales-team acceptance
  • Set pilot review criteria and an exit process

Frequently asked questions

Is outsourced SDR always cheaper?

No. Compare the full cost and scope of each model rather than assuming an outsourced seat always costs less.

Should an agency use white-label SDRs?

White-label capacity can fit agencies with established targeting and processes that need additional execution without giving up client ownership. See our agency partnership approach.

What should an SDR pilot measure?

Useful measures include data quality, relevant conversations, qualification accuracy, held meetings, accepted handoffs and reporting reliability.
