White-Label SDR Outsourcing Checklist for Agencies

A white-label SDR partnership should add delivery capacity while protecting the agency's client relationship and operational standards.

Clarify ownership before launch

Document who owns the end-client relationship, messaging, domains, contact lists, CRM records, reporting, intellectual property and exit procedures.

Set qualification standards

Agree target account profile, excluded accounts, decision-maker criteria, handoff format and how an agency accepts or rejects a meeting.

Define operating boundaries

Specify which party supplies the CRM, dialer, outreach domains, prospect data, scripts, training and compliance review.

Make reporting actionable

Track activity, conversations, meeting acceptance, attendance, quality findings and reasons for lost opportunities. Share the agreed scorecard with the agency.

Protect continuity

Plan coverage, escalation, access revocation, documentation, replacement training and a structured handover at the end of the engagement.
