FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does white-label SDR outsourcing work?

An agency retains the client relationship, target accounts, campaign strategy and brand. MGI provides scoped delivery capacity using approved workflows, scripts, tools and reporting.

Who controls the CRM and campaign data?

The client or agency defines access, data ownership, permissions and retention requirements in the engagement agreement.

Can we begin with a small pilot?

Yes. Pilot size, coverage, training and performance review criteria are agreed according to campaign needs and capacity.
