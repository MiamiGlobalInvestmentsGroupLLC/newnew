FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do you define a qualified meeting?

Qualification criteria are agreed in advance and may include account fit, contact role, expressed needs and clear next-step expectations.

Are booked meeting numbers guaranteed?

No. Campaign performance depends on targeting, prospect data, messaging, the offer and market conditions. Agreed pilot metrics help measure results.

Who supplies the outreach tools and contact lists?

Responsibilities for approved contact data, CRM, calling tools, calendar access and outreach compliance are confirmed during scoping.
