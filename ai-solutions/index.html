What we are designing

SOFTWARE INFORMED BY REAL OPERATIONS.

Our product direction starts with operating problems common to distributed service delivery: fragmented tools, manual handoffs, inconsistent follow-up, limited visibility and repetitive coordination.

01 / CUSTOMER ENGAGEMENT

AI-assisted communications

Planned support for conversation assistance, response guidance, appointment workflows and structured follow-up with defined escalation and human-review rules.

02 / WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

From repetitive tasks to managed flows

Planned automation for lead routing, task creation, handoffs, reminders and recurring operational steps across connected business systems.

03 / OPERATIONS INTELLIGENCE

One view of execution

A product direction for bringing activity, workflow status, QA signals and management reporting into a clearer operating layer.

04 / HUMAN + AI

Automation with oversight

The design goal is to combine automation with approvals, escalation paths, role permissions and human review rather than remove accountability.

Engineering direction

CLOUD-NATIVE, API-FIRST, DESIGNED TO SCALE.

The proposed MVP architecture is being evaluated around modular cloud services so capabilities can be developed independently and scaled if product validation supports broader use.

AI layerFoundation-model orchestration, retrieval patterns and guarded business workflows.
Serverless executionEvent-driven functions and APIs for variable workloads and faster product iteration.
Data & observabilitySecure application data, operational events, logging, metrics and audit-oriented visibility.
Cloud architecture evaluation

AWS IS PART OF OUR CURRENT TECHNICAL EVALUATION.

For a future MVP and scale path, MGI is evaluating AWS services that fit the intended architecture, including Amazon Bedrock for generative AI, AWS Lambda and API Gateway for serverless application logic, Amazon Cognito for identity, Amazon DynamoDB or Aurora for application data, Amazon S3 for object storage, and CloudWatch/EventBridge for observability and event-driven workflows.

Development disclosure: the platform is currently in pre-MVP product design. The AWS services listed above describe architecture under evaluation and are not a claim that those services are already deployed in production. Final architecture, product scope and release timing remain subject to engineering validation and business decisions.
