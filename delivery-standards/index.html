Our delivery approach

ACCOUNTABILITY IS BUILT INTO THE WORKFLOW.

Our delivery framework explains how a managed outsourcing engagement can be structured. Specific services, responsibilities, service levels and reporting are agreed in writing for each client.

01 — Discovery and scope

We map business objectives, workflows, channels, hours, language requirements, systems, escalation points and success measures before proposing a delivery model.

02 — Candidate sourcing and selection

We identify candidates against role requirements and coordinate relevant screening and client review. We do not represent unassigned candidates as a deployed team.

03 — Onboarding and controlled access

We align process documentation, training, approved scripts, system permissions and escalation contacts before go-live.

04 — Daily management and quality assurance

Depending on the agreement, managed delivery may include attendance monitoring, coaching, workflow checks, call or ticket reviews, and documented issue resolution.

05 — Reporting and continuous improvement

We agree relevant KPIs such as first-response time, contact quality, CRM hygiene, qualified appointments, schedule adherence or resolution rates. Targets depend on the work and are not universal guarantees.

Sample weekly performance review

Illustrative template — not actual client performance: Work completed; attendance and coverage; quality checks; KPI trends; escalations; corrective actions; priorities for the next week. This format can be tailored to your systems and workflow.

What clients should expect

Clear scope, named communication channels, defined escalation routes and agreed reporting cadence. Pricing, replacement procedures and responsibilities are governed by the signed agreement, not generic website promises.
