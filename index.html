FAQ

STRAIGHT ANSWERS BEFORE WE TALK.

The model is flexible, but the operating expectations should be clear.

What does MGI do?

MGI provides dedicated outsourced capacity for sales-development, customer-support and back-office workflows, with a white-label model for agency partners.

How does white-label delivery work?

Your agency retains the client relationship, strategy, brand, campaign direction and technology stack. MGI provides execution capacity aligned to the operating model you define.

Can MGI work inside our existing CRM and dialer?

Where appropriate and approved, yes. Teams can operate within existing systems, permissions, scripts and reporting structures.

Can we begin with a small pilot?

Yes. A controlled pilot can align the workflow, validate the operating model and define the path to scale.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is customized based on volume, scope, hours, skill requirements, campaign complexity and dedicated seats.

Where is MGI registered?

Miami Global Investments Group LLC is a Florida-registered limited liability company.
