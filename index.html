Common questions

BUSINESS PROCESS OUTSOURCING, EXPLAINED.

What is managed BPO?

Managed business process outsourcing means a partner runs an agreed business workflow, including staffing, coordination, quality reviews and reporting, rather than only supplying workers.

Can we outsource customer support?

Yes. MGI can scope managed customer service outsourcing for phone, email and chat, including defined escalation paths and quality measures.

Can you manage back-office work?

We can scope back-office outsourcing for CRM administration, data entry, research and recurring administrative processes.

Can we start with a small team?

Yes. We can discuss a limited pilot before expanding the team and responsibilities based on performance and fit.
