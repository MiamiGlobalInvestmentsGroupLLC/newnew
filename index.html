About Miami Global Investments Group LLC

MGI is a boutique advisory firm based in Miami, Florida. We specialize in commercial lending, credit structuring, SME banking advisory, and risk/compliance support grounded in real-world banking experience.

Our advisory work is shaped by hands-on underwriting, portfolio growth, financial analysis, collateral review, and regulatory frameworks including OCC, FDIC, CFPB, BSA/AML, KYC, and Fair Lending. We bridge the gap between business owners and lenders with strategy that is practical, credible, and lender-ready.

Whether you are preparing your first bank financing request or managing a complex lending portfolio, we deliver insights that improve credit outcomes and drive responsible growth.

What we bring:

  • Deep domain expertise in commercial and SME lending
  • Credibility with banks and lenders – we speak their language
  • Practical, implementation-focused advisory

What We Do

Comprehensive advisory services designed to make credit decisions clearer, faster, and more confident for SMEs and lenders.
