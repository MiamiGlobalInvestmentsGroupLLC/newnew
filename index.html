Frequently asked questions

THE DETAILS BEHIND A MANAGED OUTSOURCING PARTNERSHIP.

Clear answers about team management, accountability, systems, quality, pricing and launch expectations.

What does MGI manage beyond recruiting?

MGI can coordinate recruitment, onboarding, schedules, workflow handoffs, daily supervision, quality reviews and performance reporting under an agreed scope. The exact responsibilities are documented before launch.

Which business processes can MGI support?

Our focus includes customer support, appointment setting, outbound calling, white-label SDR execution, CRM administration, data entry, research and back-office operations. We scope each engagement to the client's actual needs.

How is MGI different from a staffing agency?

A staffing agency primarily supplies candidates. MGI's managed outsourcing model can include operational oversight, coaching, quality assurance and reporting in addition to talent sourcing, subject to the service agreement.

Can we start with one agent or a pilot team?

Yes. A focused pilot or a small dedicated team can be discussed. Team size, launch schedule and scope depend on role requirements and candidate availability.

Do you offer white-label SDR teams for agencies?

Yes. MGI can support agency-led campaigns behind the agency's brand, with agreed workflows, approved messaging, CRM usage, handoffs and reporting. The agency retains ownership of its client relationships and strategy.

Can MGI handle inbound customer service and outbound calls?

We can scope phone, email and chat support as well as outbound appointment setting. Channel coverage, working hours, scripts and escalation rules are agreed with each client.

How do you measure quality and performance?

We define relevant KPIs before launch. Depending on the workflow, these may include response times, QA scores, appointment quality, CRM accuracy, attendance, resolution rates and scheduled performance reviews.

Will the team use our CRM, dialer or helpdesk?

Where access and security requirements permit, team members can work in the client's approved systems. Tool access, permissions, training and integration needs are confirmed during onboarding.

How do you protect customer information?

Data handling, least-privilege access, confidentiality requirements and escalation procedures should be documented before launch. We do not claim any particular security certification unless independently verified.

Are you able to support regulated healthcare or financial workflows?

Potentially, but only after reviewing the workflow, applicable requirements, contractual obligations and safeguards. No HIPAA, PCI or other regulatory compliance certification is implied by this website.

Do you guarantee appointments, revenue or cost savings?

No responsible provider can guarantee commercial outcomes without understanding the offer, market, data, campaign and operating conditions. We agree on measurable delivery expectations and review results transparently.

How much does managed outsourcing cost?

Pricing depends on role complexity, language requirements, coverage hours, headcount, tools, supervision and reporting. Request a tailored proposal for a clearly defined scope.

Where are MGI and its teams located?

Miami Global Investments Group LLC is registered in Florida, with a Miami mailing address. Its service model uses distributed delivery; the location and coverage of each proposed team are discussed with the client.

How can I verify MGI as a business?

Search the Florida Division of Corporations registry for Miami Global Investments Group LLC, document number L25000313045. The official company registration confirms legal status, not client results or third-party endorsements.

What happens after we request a proposal?

We review your process, staffing needs, hours, systems, quality criteria and expected outcomes; then discuss a scope, candidate sourcing, onboarding and a pilot or rollout plan. No team is represented as already assigned before confirmation.
